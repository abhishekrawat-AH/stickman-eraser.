# Edit Guide (CapCut / DaVinci Resolve)

1. **Project:** 1080x1920, 30 fps.
2. **Assemble** clips per `02-storyboard/storyboard.md`. Trim each to its beat.
3. **Line boil:** keep clip motion on 2s (12 fps look). Add a subtle paper-flicker overlay at 10–15% opacity.
4. **Captions:** big bold hand-lettered style; under 5 words; keep in the safe zone (away from the bottom 20% UI).
5. **Cuts:** a cut or push-in every 1–2 seconds.
6. **Loop:** make the last frame match shot 1; crossfade the audio tail into the opening thud.
7. **Audio:** place SFX per `04-sound/sfx-cue-sheet.md`; check levels on phone speakers.
8. **Export:** H.264 MP4, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 10–12 Mbps, AAC 192 kbps, under 60 seconds.
9. Save as `assets/exports/stickman-eraser-v1.mp4`.
