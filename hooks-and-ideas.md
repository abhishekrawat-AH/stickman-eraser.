# Hooks, Ideas, Schedule

## Hook bank (first 2 seconds)
- Looming shadow before the reveal
- "He drew a door… 😭" text
- Start mid-chase (skip any intro)
- Freeze on a shocked face, then cut to action
- Loop ending that matches the opening frame

## Retention rules
- No intro, no logo, no talking
- Escalate every beat; a new surprise every 3–6s
- End on a twist that invites a rewatch
