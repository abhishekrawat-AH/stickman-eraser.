# Concept: Stickman vs Giant Eraser

**Logline:** A tiny pencil stickman is hunted across a notebook page by a giant eraser. Every escape he draws gets erased, until he draws a bigger eraser.

**Format:** YouTube Short, 9:16, 40–50 seconds, no dialogue.
**Tone:** Mischievous, slapstick, family-friendly.

## Character: Stickman
- Round head, thin graphite limbs, two big dot eyes, tiny expressive eyebrows
- Pencil-gray only; no color
- Big pantomime reactions: gulp, sweat drop, double-take

## Antagonist: The Eraser
- Pink/white rubber block drawn in the same sketch style (only splash of color allowed: faint pink)
- Never speaks; menace comes from sound (squeak-rub) and a looming shadow

## World rules
1. Everything the stickman draws becomes real instantly.
2. The eraser can remove anything drawn, including parts of the stickman if it touches him.
3. The page edge is the "outside": no escaping off-page.
4. Final twist: scale beats speed. He draws a bigger eraser.

## Visual style
Graphite rough lines, visible paper grain, slight line boil (frame-to-frame jitter) on 2s.
Background: lined notebook paper, off-white (#F4F1EA).

## Loop ending
Last frame = giant eraser shadow sweeping in again, matching the first frame so the Short loops seamlessly.
